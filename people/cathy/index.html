The Porch

Cathy Holloway

“I woke up dead. Somehow I still have to deal with all the usual bullshit.”

Look, I’m not great at sitting still and I’m definitely not great at pretending everything is normal. If I’m out on the porch before the street wakes up, I’ve got a mug of coffee, too many thoughts, and a pretty low tolerance for anyone telling me to calm down.

Pull up a chair if you want. Just don’t ask me to explain the whole backstory before I’ve finished my sentence.
