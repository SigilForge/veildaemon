# SigilForge Studios — Executive Overview (Public)

> **Open-web summary only.** Not the private SigilForge archive. Full diligence materials are shared by request, not linked here.

**Version:** Public · July 2026 · updated October 18, 2026

## What it is

A tech-enabled mythpunk studio using one governed story-and-rules framework to produce fiction, tabletop products, live software, and eventually location-based interactive experiences.

## What already exists

- Shipping free and paid tabletop products
- Book One direct digital on the Published Shelf (PDF, EPUB, MOBI + wallpaper pack)
- Operator/Handler play surfaces with deliberate Cell sync
- VeilLink identity, ownership, Live-Link sign-in, and dynamic links
- Digital shelf previews (dossiers, wallpapers)
- Creator Rights registry with public record routes, AI permission declarations, JSON metadata, and QR-linked verification
- Internally audited rules corpus and multi-book manuscripts
- Public wiki and marketing library
- Governed production pipeline with human release authority
- $0 paid acquisition through the July 10, 2026 traction snapshot

## Commercial sequence

1. **Near-term:** tabletop core + Needlepoints + print-on-demand editions (crowdfunding optional)
2. **Mid-term:** premium VeilDaemon content + events
3. **Upside:** Mobile AR after audience and IP pipeline

## Commercial launch sequence

Products already sell direct (itch storefront, Book One checkout). The expanded line launches in this order:

1. **Entity formation** — a limited liability company for SigilForge Studios (planned; not yet formed)
2. **EIN and payment readiness** — EIN, business banking, and storefront/payment accounts under the entity
3. **Coordinated commercial launch** — direct digital, print-on-demand editions, and storefront listings together
4. **Convention announcement** — targeted for February 2027 (a target, not a commitment)

Crowdfunding is not a step in this sequence; it remains a separate, optional financing or promotional decision.
