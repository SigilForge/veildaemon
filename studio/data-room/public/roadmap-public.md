## Sequencing principle

Compound layers: what ships now, what funding accelerates next, and what long-horizon work inherits from canon already in motion. Long-horizon interactive (including survival-scale concepts) is not presented as a current product promise.

## Layer 1 — Current commercial foundation (now)

Already shipping or live:

- Operator Core and Handler Core (paid)
- Free Needlepoint onboarding funnel
- Operator/Handler play surfaces (deliberate Cell sync)
- VeilLink identity, ownership, and Live-Link sign-in
- Book One (CradlePoint: VeilSight) direct digital — PDF, EPUB, MOBI + wallpaper pack
- Digital shelf previews
- Public archive / wiki
- Fiction line and canon library

## Commercial launch sequence

Products already sell direct (itch storefront, Book One checkout). The expanded line launches in this order:

1. **Entity formation** — a limited liability company for SigilForge Studios (planned; not yet formed)
2. **EIN and payment readiness** — EIN, business banking, and storefront/payment accounts under the entity
3. **Coordinated commercial launch** — direct digital, print-on-demand editions, and storefront listings together
4. **Convention announcement and Kickstarter** — the CradlePoint Starter is announced and its Kickstarter launches, if the campaign is production-ready; targeted for February 2027 (a target, not a commitment)

Preparation before February runs on existing resources and any separately confirmed funding. It does not assume Kickstarter proceeds or the $67,000 budget. Print-on-demand sales do not depend on the campaign.

## Layer 2 — Funded publishing growth (12–24 months)

Funding accelerates:

- Weekly production cadence with monthly public release target (not a claim of one complete commercial module every week)
- Professional finish of flagship materials
- Storefront, print-on-demand editions, conventions, creator media
- Structured progression-year releases

## Layer 3 — ARG + long-term AR / Ritual Sites (2–5 years)

Near-term funded work stays adjacent to publishing (infrastructure, hooks, companion hardening).
Persistent Mobile AR and Ritual Sites remain long-term platform vision and bounded proof-of-concept research—not near-term launch SKUs unless separately scoped and funded.

## Capital separation

- **$67K / 12 months** — first-year operating and production budget, including the CradlePoint Starter and first-year publishing program
- **Funding channels** — investors, grants, and a planned Kickstarter fund the same plan; never counted twice
- **Mobile AR MVP** — separate after technical quotes
- **≤$20K PoC** — optional bounded barrier, not studio valuation
