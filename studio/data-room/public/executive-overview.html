What it is

A tech-enabled mythpunk studio using one governed story-and-rules framework to produce fiction, tabletop products, live software, and eventually location-based interactive experiences.

What already exists

  • Shipping free and paid tabletop products
  • Book One direct digital on the Published Shelf (PDF, EPUB, MOBI + wallpaper pack)
  • Operator/Handler play surfaces with deliberate Cell sync
  • VeilLink identity, ownership, Live-Link sign-in, and dynamic links
  • Digital shelf previews (dossiers, wallpapers)
  • Creator Rights registry with public record routes, AI permission declarations, JSON metadata, and QR-linked verification
  • Internally audited rules corpus and multi-book manuscripts
  • Public wiki and marketing library
  • Governed production pipeline with human release authority
  • $0 paid acquisition through the July 10, 2026 traction snapshot

Commercial sequence

Near-term: tabletop core + Needlepoints + print-on-demand editions; CradlePoint Starter Kickstarter planned

Mid-term: premium VeilDaemon content + events

Upside: Mobile AR after audience and IP pipeline

Commercial launch sequence

Products already sell direct (itch storefront, Book One checkout). The expanded line launches in this order:

  1. Entity formation — a limited liability company for SigilForge Studios (planned; not yet formed)
  2. EIN and payment readiness — EIN, business banking, and storefront/payment accounts under the entity
  3. Coordinated commercial launch — direct digital, print-on-demand editions, and storefront listings together
  4. Convention announcement and Kickstarter — the CradlePoint Starter is announced and its Kickstarter launches, if the campaign is production-ready; targeted for February 2027 (a target, not a commitment)

Preparation before February runs on existing resources and any separately confirmed funding. It does not assume Kickstarter proceeds or the $67,000 budget. Print-on-demand sales do not depend on the campaign.

Funding (public)

  • $67,000 / 12 months first-year operating and production budget, covering the CradlePoint Starter and first-year publishing program (capacity, art, specialists, infrastructure, publishing and physical distribution, video, conventions, fiction)
  • Investors and funding partners, grants, and a planned Kickstarter are channels toward this one plan; committed funding reduces what remains
  • A Kickstarter goal adds the campaign’s own costs (fees, rewards, shipping, taxes, contingency) on top of the budget
  • The Mobile AR MVP is a separate scoped budget
  • Optional ≤$20K bounded proof-of-concept contribution is not the studio plan
