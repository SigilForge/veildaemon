Development record

This page is newer than most of what it describes. The Archive had been changing in public for months before Studio News existed, so each entry here is dated to when it actually happened, not to when someone wrote it down. The record starts on May 16, 2026, the earliest point with a continuous public trail. Newest first.

  1. SEP 25 2026

    Book One becomes CradlePoint: VeilSight

    Book One is now CradlePoint: VeilSight, rebuilt as a v48 second edition with a new cover, new title matter, and a device readout that finally says VEILSIGHT: ACTIVE. The prose is unchanged. Buyers get the full bundle, including PDF, EPUB, MOBI, and a re-lettered wallpaper pack, and its Creator Rights Record keeps its permanent URL, adding version 2 instead of rewriting version 1.

    Read the full brief →

    Book One · Publishing · Creator Rights · Book One shelf · Rights record
  2. SEP 24–25 2026

    Brand state locked in

    Every surface now wears the mark that owns it. SigilForge Studios on the Studio pages, the registry, Creator Rights, and VeilLink. VeilCorp on the VeilDaemon terminal. CradlePoint only where a page is explicitly about CradlePoint. The last "Cradlepoint Studio" remnants were removed, the press kit was rebuilt from the real masters, and the Witness Key's WE WITNESS ring was corrected.

    The next morning the Creator Rights rendering pipeline was repaired, and a check that fails closed now stops the public rights pages from quietly diverging from their records.

    Read the full brief →

    Brand · Creator Rights · Press room · Registry
  3. SEP 20–22 2026

    Storefront and VeilLink reorganized

    VeilLink became an umbrella portal, with /home as the signed-in hub instead of a page pretending to be a single product. The SigilForge storefront got clear paths for Book One, the TTRPG, Operator and Handler, VeilLink, and VeilDaemon. Web design became a real offer, with fixed packages, direct checkout, and a text-message contact route for people who do not want another form.

    Read the full brief →

    Storefront · VeilLink · Web design · Catalog
  4. SEP 19–23 2026

    Daily commentary finds its shape

    After the first Dispatch, a daily rhythm settled in. Each piece gives one character, speaking through one Frequency, a reaction to something happening in the real world: KIRA // VOID, KAELYN // BECOMING, ALEX // SILENCE, WEDNESDAY // BECOMING.

    Archive Notes become a publishing cadence

    Father Samiel on the 12th, Alex on the 13th, Diana on the 14th, the first weekly Dispatch on the 15th, and Wednesday on the 16th. Each note started as something small enough to pass through a social feed and then followed it into the Archive. This is the point where characters speaking through the Archive became a recognizable public format instead of a one-off. Every note opens free.

A table no longer has to share a screen to share a session.

Development history · not public releases

Before the Archive opened

The project is two years older than its public record. None of these were announcements. They are here so the dates above do not look like spontaneous generation. Oldest first. Read the full brief →

  1. MAY 2024

    Project inception

    The earliest date the project claims for itself. The preserved beta manuscript still carries it in its front matter: Original Project Inception: May 2024.

  2. JUN 2025

    Resonance of the Void

    Book One's draft era, under its first title, Resonance of the Void. The same beta manuscript is stamped Draft Version: June 2025.

  3. JUN 24 – JUL 2 2025

    From Resonance of the Void to CradlePoint

    On June 24 the book and its series began moving to a new identity: The CradlePoint Archives: The Anchor and the Glitch. By June 28 the old Resonance of the Void – Book 1 file was explicitly treated as the obsolete beta. On June 29 the CradlePoint Archives workspace was active. On July 2 a newer full CradlePoint manuscript was uploaded as its replacement.

    Read the full brief →

  4. JUL 2 2025

    VeilDaemon repository created

    The same day the new manuscript arrived, the VeilDaemon repository was created. A cloud project followed on July 13, and Pages deployments were running by August 6. In August it was mostly a Python toolkit for stream persona work, with an event bus, a stage director, and text-to-speech.

  5. OCT 29 2025

    The manuscript, in working copies

    The Book One manuscript exists as a working document by this date. A Scrivener project, still carrying the old Resonance of the Void name, was created on November 9, and edits continued there into May 2026. By November 12 there was also an EPUB.

  6. NOV 21 2025

    The TTRPG gets a repository

    The CradlePoint TTRPG repository was created. Two days later it already had a resonance arc and emotional-weather tools for the Handler.

  7. DEC 6 2025

    CradlePoint crosses into VeilForge

    VeilForge, the studio's local AI runtime, took in CradlePoint message packs for its terminal interface. It is the first commit where the universe and the tooling started contaminating each other.

  8. FEB 14 2026

    The TTRPG corpus

    The ontology, Player Handbook v1.1, the Monster Manual, and the larger Complete Books corpus were all present and being indexed. The system existed three months before anyone outside could see it.
