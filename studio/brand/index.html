Brand Hierarchy

SigilForge Studios is the real-world publishing studio and business entity. CradlePoint Universe is the overarching speculative narrative and IP setting within which products and live software operate.

SigilForge Studios (Real-world publishing studio)
└── CradlePoint Universe (Speculative IP universe & setting)
    ├── VeilDaemon (Shade's intake, indexing, & triage node)
    ├── VeilForge (Local-first AI assistant & engine)
    ├── VeilLink (Dynamic table live-link & account layer)
    └── RelayDaemon (Live synthesis & dispatch engine)

Usage Scenarios

  • Real-world business: Always use SigilForge Studios for entity attribution, contracts, press releases, licensing, and footer copyright lines.
  • Narrative & IP: Use CradlePoint Universe when describing the shared setting across tabletop products, novels, and interactive media.
  • Diegetic software: VeilCorp is a fictional in-universe entity. Its marks belong inside the setting, never as a real-world contracting party.
