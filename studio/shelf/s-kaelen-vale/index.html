The author

Signal, noise, and the people who notice first

S. Kaelen Vale (also styled S. Kaelën Vale) writes the CradlePoint Universe: myth-tech horror from Kansas City, Missouri, where emotion behaves like physics, old buildings keep their own records, and the people who notice first are almost never the people in charge.

Vale came up through telecommunications and fraud investigation, and it shows. The CradlePoint books are built like incident reports that learned to dream: signal, noise, and the institutions that would prefer you never learned to tell them apart.

Vale publishes independently through SigilForge Studios. No gatekeepers, no house style, all signal.
